1. REFLECTED XSS

* Concept: Why does this attack disappear when you refresh the page?

3. CROSS-SITE REQUEST FORGERY (CSRF)
* Malicious URL Crafted: [<img src="http://127.0.0.1:8081/transfer?to=Attacker&amount=5000" style="display:none;">]
* Concept: What is the primary defense against CSRF?
Answer: Token can be used since the server generates them and they are unique and cryptographically secure and the token is saved in the user's session's data.
